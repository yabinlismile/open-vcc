You fund a user's debit card by converting USDT from your institution wallet into the card's fiat currency. ByCard supports two fee deduction modes: internal (fee subtracted from the deposit amount) and external (fee charged on top of the deposit amount). Choose the mode that best fits your pricing model.

## Deposit modes

ByCard offers two deposit endpoints. The difference is how the loading fee is applied:

| Mode | Endpoint | How fees work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Internal deduction** | `POST /api/v1/deposit-transactions` | The fee is taken from the deposit amount. For example, depositing 100 USD with a 1% fee loads 99 USD onto the card and deducts 100 USDT from your wallet. |
| **External deduction** | `POST /api/v1/deposit-transactions/external-deduction` | The fee is added on top of the deposit amount. For example, depositing 100 USD with a 1% fee loads 100 USD onto the card and deducts 101 USDT from your wallet. |

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ensure sufficient wallet balance">
    Verify that your institution wallet holds enough USDT or RUSD to cover the deposit amount plus any fees. You can check your balance through the [institution balance query API](/api/institution/balance_query).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the deduction mode">
    Select internal or external deduction based on your user experience and pricing agreement. Use the corresponding endpoint from the table above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the deposit request">
    Send a `POST` request with the following required fields:

    | Field | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | `card_no` | The card ID returned when the card was issued |
    | `acct_no` | Your unique user ID |
    | `amount` | The deposit amount as a string, up to 10 digits with a maximum of 2 decimal places (single transaction maximum is 10,000 unless otherwise agreed) |
    | `coin_type` | The deposit currency: `USDT` or `RUSD` |
    | `cust_tx_id` | A unique transaction ID you generate (max 64 chars) |

    Optional fields include `remark` and `card_currency` (for dual-currency cards).

    <CodeGroup>

    ```json Request
    {
      "acct_no": "tevau_uat_001",
      "amount": "0.92",
      "card_no": "26614057422630000012874",
      "coin_type": "USDT",
      "cust_tx_id": "IN31e12f11f50c4bb49dc9f1b60eec9328",
      "remark": "充值-走外扣手续费"
    }
    ```

    ```json Response
    {
      "code": 0,
      "msg": "SUCCESS",
      "result": {
        "tx_id": "2020022511324811001637548",
        "coin_type": "USDT",
        "tx_amount": "0.92",
        "exchange_fee_rate": "0",
        "exchange_fee": "0",
        "loading_fee": "0.0812",
        "deposit_usdt": "0.9188",
        "currency_type": "USD",
        "currency_amount": "0.92",
        "exchange_rate": "1.00239251357",
        "fiat_exchange_rate": "1"
      }
    }
    ```

    </CodeGroup>

    The response fields include:

    - `tx_id`: ByCard's internal transaction ID
    - `deposit_usdt`: The amount actually credited to the card after fees, in `coin_type` units
    - `loading_fee`: The platform loading fee, in `coin_type` units
    - `exchange_fee`: The currency conversion fee, in `coin_type` units
    - `currency_amount`: The fiat amount loaded onto the card
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await the deposit webhook confirmation">
    After the API returns success, wait for the [card deposit webhook](/api/webhook/card_deposit_events) to confirm the final transaction status on the blockchain and banking side.
  </Step>
</Steps>
